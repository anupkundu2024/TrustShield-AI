How to check a suspicious screenshot

  1. Choose a clear PNG or JPEG up to 5 MB. Keep the relevant website address and message text in view.
  2. Cover identification numbers, payment details, passwords and verification codes before you upload.
  3. Review the red flags, then verify the sender, website, or payment request through an official channel.

What the scanner can flag

  • Phishing indicators such as a look-alike domain or a link that conflicts with the organization named in the image.
  • Requests for an upfront fee, OTP, login credentials, or an unexpected download.
  • Possible impersonation, or visible inconsistencies around a name, amount, timestamp or logo.

Can a photo scan prove an image is real?

A visual scan can identify cues worth checking. Edited and genuine images can look similar, and compression or cropping can make text unclear. For a payment or job offer, check the original message and contact the organization using independently verified details.

If you need a reputation check, enter the visible address separately in the URL Scanner. The image scanner does not open links or decode QR codes.
