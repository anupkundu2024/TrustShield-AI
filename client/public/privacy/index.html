Privacy at TrustShield AI

Accounts and sessions

Signing up creates an account with your name, email address and a password hash. A session cookie keeps you signed in. The service uses account and scan request limits to protect availability.

Scans and saved reports

When you opt in to AI analysis, your submission is sent to OpenAI. If you opt in to a URL reputation lookup, URLhaus receives the web address without credentials, query parameters or fragments. Those providers apply their own data handling policies.

TrustShield AI stores your report and the account it belongs to. When Cloudinary storage is enabled, the original image or PDF is stored as an authenticated asset until you delete its report. Reports may describe details from your submission, so cover personal information first. You can delete your reports from Scan History.

Questions and account requests

The operator of this site handles privacy questions and account requests.
